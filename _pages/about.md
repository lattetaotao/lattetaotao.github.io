# About Me

I am **Tao Wang**, a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from **Universiti Malaya**. My research focuses on **nonlinear time-series analysis, entropy-based signal characterization, and intelligent fault diagnosis of rotating machinery**.

During my doctoral research, I developed a series of entropy-based complexity measures for vibration signal analysis, including **Distance Similarity Entropy (DSEN)**, **Multi-scale Distance Similarity Entropy (MDSE)**, and **Dual-hierarchical Multi-scale Distance Similarity Entropy (DHMDSEn)**. These methods aim to improve the characterization of nonlinear and non-stationary vibration signals for intelligent condition monitoring and fault diagnosis.

My research also explores **generative modeling, data augmentation, and intelligent diagnosis under imbalanced and limited-data conditions**, with particular interests in improving the reliability and generalization ability of machine-learning-based fault diagnosis methods.

My work has been published in journals including *Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing*, *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*, *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*, and *Measurement*. For a complete publication list, please visit my <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=TvivfDYAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=sra" target="_blank">Google Scholar profile</a>.

# 🔬 Research Interests

- Nonlinear time-series analysis and complexity measures
- Entropy-based vibration feature extraction
- Intelligent fault diagnosis and condition monitoring
- Rotating machinery health monitoring
- Generative modeling and data augmentation
- Few-shot and limited-data learning


## Research Highlights

### Entropy-based Complexity Analysis

Development of nonlinear complexity measures for vibration signal characterization, including **DSEN**, **MDSE**, and **DHMDSEn**, with applications to bearing, gearbox, and wind turbine gearbox fault diagnosis.

### Intelligent Rotating Machinery Diagnosis

Development of robust feature-extraction and machine-learning frameworks for condition monitoring and fault diagnosis under noise, variable operating conditions, and complex industrial environments.

### Learning with Limited Fault Data

Investigation of generative models, data augmentation, and limited-data learning strategies for intelligent diagnosis under imbalanced and scarce fault-data conditions.
